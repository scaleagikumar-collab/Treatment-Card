Go to the hospital now if

  • Fever — at or above 100.4°F. Do not give paracetamol and wait. Go now.
  • Bleeding that will not stop — nose, gums, in vomit, in stool or urine
  • Breathing that is fast, noisy or difficult
  • Very drowsy, confused, or cannot be woken properly
  • A fit or seizure
  • The line or port site red, swollen, painful or leaking
  • Not passing urine, not drinking, or vomiting again and again
  • Anything else your doctor told you to call about

The basics
Display

Plain keeps a neutral title on your calendar and lock screen. Detailed shows what it is, and the name if set.

Language

Reading and colour

A face made by the Braille Institute for low vision. It pulls apart the letters and numbers that are easiest to confuse — 0 and O, 1 and l, b and d.

Given, missed and vomited are told apart by colour. If reds and greens look alike to you, this switches to a palette that stays distinct and adds a mark to each one.

On-screen privacy

The name at the top is visible to anyone who glances at your phone — in a queue, at the school gate. This keeps it off the screen without removing it from your records or reports.
